# Privacy Policy for Strata

**Effective date: 09/13/2026**

Strata is an Android launcher developed and published by **You Know Its Me Studios**.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Strata accesses, how that information is used, and when information may be sent to third-party services.

## Summary

Strata is designed to keep user configuration and launcher data on the user's device.

Strata does not:

- sell personal information
- display advertisements
- use advertising trackers
- require a Strata account
- collect analytics for advertising purposes
- upload the user's launcher configuration to You Know Its Me Studios

Some optional Strata features require access to Android system information or third-party services in order to function.

---

## Information Stored on Your Device

Strata may store the following information locally on your device:

- Favorites and app groups
- app ordering and group membership
- launcher layout and density settings
- Glance Deck layout and module configuration
- selected themes and custom theme settings
- navigation and search preferences
- language preferences
- weather location preferences
- cached weather information
- cached application names and icons
- battery-module preferences
- other launcher configuration selected by the user

This information is used to restore your launcher quickly and preserve your preferences between sessions.

This information is not automatically uploaded to You Know Its Me Studios.

---

## Installed Applications

Strata is an Android launcher and therefore needs to identify applications that can be launched on your device.

Strata may access information such as:

- application name
- application icon
- package name
- launchable activity/component
- application shortcuts exposed by Android

This information is used to display and launch applications inside Strata.

Installed-app information is processed locally on your device and is not sent to You Know Its Me Studios.

---

## Location

Strata's Weather feature may use approximate device location if the user chooses **Device Location**.

Strata does not require precise location for weather functionality.

Location may be used to:

- retrieve local weather forecasts
- determine the city or general locality displayed in the Weather module

When Weather is enabled, approximate coordinates may be transmitted to the weather provider described below.

Users may instead configure a manual weather location.

Strata does not use location for advertising, profiling, or tracking.

---

## Weather Data

Strata currently uses the **MET Norway Weather API** to retrieve weather forecasts.

When weather information is requested, information sent to the weather service may include:

- approximate latitude and longitude
- technical request information required to retrieve the forecast

The user's IP address may also be visible to the weather provider as part of normal Internet communication.

Weather information may be cached locally on the device to improve performance and reduce unnecessary network requests.

MET Norway operates independently of You Know Its Me Studios and is subject to its own terms and privacy practices.

Website:

https://api.met.no/

---

## Calendar Access

If the user enables calendar-related Glance Deck features, Strata may request permission to read calendar information.

Calendar access may be used to display information such as:

- upcoming events
- event time
- event title
- calendar information
- event location, when available

Calendar information is processed locally on the device.

Strata does not upload calendar contents to You Know Its Me Studios.

Users may deny or revoke calendar permission at any time through Android settings.

---

## Media Information

Some Strata features may access Android media-session information when the user grants the required system access.

This may include:

- currently playing media
- media title
- playback state
- media application
- available playback controls

This information is used to display and control media from Strata.

Media information is not uploaded to You Know Its Me Studios.

---

## Bluetooth and Connected Devices

Strata may use Android Bluetooth or connected-device APIs for optional features such as displaying battery information for supported connected accessories.

Depending on the device and Android version, Strata may access:

- connected-device identity
- connection state
- publicly exposed accessory battery information

This information is processed locally and is not uploaded to You Know Its Me Studios.

---

## Camera and Flashlight

Strata may access Android camera hardware information to provide flashlight controls.

This access is used only for flashlight functionality, including supported flashlight intensity levels.

Strata does not capture, store, or transmit photos or video as part of this functionality.

---

## Wallpaper

When enabled through Strata's theme behavior, Strata may set the device's:

- Home-screen wallpaper
- Lock-screen wallpaper

Strata generates these wallpapers from the currently selected Strata theme background.

Wallpaper images are generated and applied locally on the device.

They are not uploaded to You Know Its Me Studios.

---

## Network Access

Strata uses Internet access only where required by features that depend on online services, such as weather information.

Most launcher functionality operates locally on the device.

---

## Data Sharing

You Know Its Me Studios does not sell user data.

Strata does not share personal information with advertisers or data brokers.

Information may be transmitted to third-party services only when necessary to provide a user-requested feature, such as sending approximate location coordinates to the weather provider to obtain a forecast.

---

## Data Retention

Launcher configuration, cached application metadata, and other preferences remain on the device until they are:

- changed by the user
- cleared through Strata
- cleared through Android application settings
- removed when Strata is uninstalled

Cached data may be refreshed or deleted automatically when it becomes outdated.

---

## Permissions

Strata only requests Android permissions needed for features the user chooses to use.

Depending on enabled features and Android version, these permissions may include access related to:

- approximate location
- calendar
- Bluetooth / connected devices
- camera / flashlight hardware
- notification or media-session access
- wallpaper management

Users can review or revoke Android permissions at any time through the device's system settings.

Some Strata features may stop functioning if their required permission is revoked.

---

## Children

Strata is not specifically directed toward children.

Strata does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

---

## Security

Strata is designed to minimize collection of personal information and keep launcher configuration on the user's device whenever possible.

No software system can guarantee absolute security, but You Know Its Me Studios takes reasonable measures to limit unnecessary data collection and exposure.
